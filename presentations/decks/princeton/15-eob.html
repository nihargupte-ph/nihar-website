\( r = \dfrac{1}{u_p(1+e\cos\zeta)} \) \( \left[\dfrac{\partial \hat H_{\rm EOB}}{\partial r}\right]_0 = -\big[\dot p_r(p_\phi, e, \zeta)\big]_0 \)
